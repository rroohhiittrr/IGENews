## 1. Module Purpose

The Communities module is a professional network for trade professionals within the IGEN ecosystem — connecting SMEs, Readers, Leaders, Exporters, Importers, and Service Providers. It is designed to build engagement, increase retention, and drive premium community memberships.

## 5. Community Strategy

The Communities module serves as the retention engine for iGEN News — keeping users engaged through:
- **Content** (discussions, expert insights)
- **Connection** (matchmaking, directories)
- **Competition** (leaderboards, badges)
- **Commerce** (trade leads, RFQs, events)
