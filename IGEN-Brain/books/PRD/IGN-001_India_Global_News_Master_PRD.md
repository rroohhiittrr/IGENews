## 1. Module Overview

**India Global News (iGEN News)** is the core content and intelligence platform within the IGEN World Ecosystem. It is a B2B trade & business news hub providing sector-specific, country-specific, company-specific, leader-specific, and expert-driven news and intelligence to business professionals, MSMEs, enterprises, investors, exporters, importers, researchers, government bodies, students, and media.

### Core Purpose
- Deliver real-time, curated, AI-powered trade and business news
- Drive premium subscription upgrades (Free → Pro → Enterprise)
- Generate advertising, sponsorship, and intelligence report revenue
- Build a global community of trade professionals

## 4. Revenue Model
