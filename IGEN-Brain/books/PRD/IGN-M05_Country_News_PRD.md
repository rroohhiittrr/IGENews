## 1. Module Purpose

The Country News Mega Menu is the central destination for country-specific business intelligence — combining news, economy, trade, investment, companies, leaders, and AI insights — while driving Pro and Enterprise subscriptions.

**Business Objective:** Increase engagement, drive premium upgrades, generate sponsorship revenue, promote companies, leaders and events, and establish iGEN News as a global country intelligence platform.

---

## 2. Target Users

Business Professionals, Exporters, Importers, Investors, Government Officials, Trade Associations, SMEs, Corporates, Researchers, Students, Media Professionals.

## 4. Submenus & Their Purposes

### 4.1 My Country
**Purpose:** Personalized country intelligence dashboard based on user's selected country (set during onboarding).

**What Users See:**
| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|

| Country Snapshot | GDP, Population, Currency, Growth |
| Today's Country News | Latest, Trending, Economy, Trade tabs |
| Economic Dashboard | GDP, Inflation, Exports, Imports |
| Trade Opportunities | Current trade leads |
| Investment Opportunities | FDI and government projects |
| Top Companies | Country's featured companies |
| Top Leaders | Country's business leaders |
| Upcoming Country Events | Expos, trade missions |
| AI Country Intelligence (Premium) | Forecasts and risk analysis |
| Premium Reports & Downloads | Country reports |

**Revenue:**

## 7. UI/UX Guidelines

- Clean dashboard layout with card-based design
- Clear visual hierarchy for economic metrics
- Premium lock indicators on gated content
- Consistent navigation across all country pages
- Strategically placed Upgrade CTAs after high-value sections
