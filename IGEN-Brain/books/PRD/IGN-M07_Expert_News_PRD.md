## 1. Module Purpose

Expert News is the central hub for discovering industry experts, reading expert insights, booking consultations, and encouraging SMEs & ASMEs to purchase Expert Pages and upgrade to premium plans.

---

## 2. Target Users

Business Owners, Companies, Industry Professionals, Investors, Researchers, Media, Government Organizations, Students, SMEs, ASMEs.

---

## 3. Mega Menu Page — Screen Sections

| Section | Description | Purpose |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Hero Banner & Search | Hero banner, search bar, filters, Register as Expert, Verify Profile, Upgrade to Enterprise CTAs | Discovery and subscription conversion |
| Featured Experts | Premium expert cards with profile, designation, company, badges, consultation CTA | Premium visibility |
| Latest Expert News Feed | Expert articles with title, author, industry, read time, CTA | Content engagement |
| Browse by Industry | Sector cards: AI, Manufacturing, Healthcare, Finance, Agriculture, Energy, Retail, Supply Chain, Export, Logistics | Easy navigation |
| Expert Spotlight | Featured premium expert with achievements and consultation CTA | Drive visibility and bookings |
| Trending Experts | Top experts by engagement | Credibility and discovery |
| Top Stories | Most-read expert articles | Engagement |
| Featured Expert Carousel | Scrollable premium profiles | Promote paid members |
| Promote Your Expertise | Upgrade banner with premium benefits | Subscription conversion |
| Right Sidebar | Rankings, webinars, reports, premium banner | Persistent conversion |

## 4. Submenus

### 4.1 SME News
**Purpose:** Dedicated news section for Subject Matter Experts.

**Sections:**
- Featured Article
- Latest News Feed
- Trending Articles
- Expert Authors
- Industry Filters
- Research Reports
- Sponsored Articles
- Newsletter

**Revenue:** Sponsored Articles, Featured Placement, Premium Publishing, Newsletter Sponsorship.

---

### 4.2 SME Pages
**Purpose:** Professional directory of SMEs.

**Sections:**
- Featured Experts
- Search & Filters
- Expert Directory
- Top Rated Experts
- Recently Joined
- Consultation CTA
- Premium Upgrade Banner

**Revenue:** Premium Profiles, Consultation Fees, Featured Listings, Verification.

---

### 4.3 By Sector (SME)
**Purpose:** Browse SMEs by industry.

**Sections:**
- Sector Categories
- Featured Experts
- Sector News
- Industry Rankings
- Upcoming Events
- Sponsored Experts

**Revenue:** Sponsored Sectors, Featured Experts, Event Promotions.

---

### 4.4 All Sector (SME)
**Purpose:** Master directory of industries for SME discovery.

**Sections:**
- Industry Directory
- Sector Statistics
- Popular Sectors
- New Sectors
- Expert Listings
- Explore Industry CTA

**Revenue:** Sponsored Categories, Industry Ads, Premium Discovery.

---

### 4.5 ASME News
**Purpose:** Publish insights from Associate Subject Matter Experts.

**Sections:**
- Featured ASME Article
- Latest News
- Emerging Expert Stories
- Learning Resources
- Trending Topics
- Upgrade Banner

**Revenue:** Sponsored Articles, Premium Publishing, Membership Upgrades.

---

### 4.6 ASME Pages
**Purpose:** Professional directory for Associate Subject Matter Experts.

**Sections:**
- Featured ASMEs
- Expert Directory
- Certifications
- Skills & Portfolio
- Mentorship
- Consultation CTA
- Upgrade Banner

**Revenue:** Premium Profiles, Certification Programs, Mentorship, Consultation Bookings.

---

### 4.7 ASME By Sector
**Purpose:** Discover Associate Experts by industry.

**Sections:**
- Sector Categories
- Emerging Experts
- Learning Resources
- Industry News
- Mentorship
- Featured ASMEs

**Revenue:** Sponsored Categories, Featured Experts, Mentorship Programs.

---

### 4.8 ASME All Sector
**Purpose:** Master directory of all Associate Experts.

**Sections:**
- Industry Directory
- Popular Skills
- Featured ASMEs
- Career Resources
- Upgrade CTA

**Revenue:** Premium Listings, Sponsored Industry Pages, Recruitment Leads.
