## 1. Module Purpose

Sector News is the central industry intelligence hub for IGEN News. It is designed for high engagement and premium conversion — helping users read news, explore sectors, purchase reports, upgrade memberships, register for events, and subscribe to newsletters.

**Business Objective:** Create a central industry intelligence hub that encourages:
- News reading & sector exploration
- Premium report purchases
- Membership upgrades
- Event registrations
- Newsletter subscriptions

---

## 2. Target Audience

Business Professionals, MSMEs, Enterprises, Investors, Exporters, Importers, Researchers, Government, Students, Media.

---

| Goal | Mechanism |
|------|-----------|
| Premium Memberships | Upgrade CTAs throughout |

| Company Pages | Featured company listings |

## 5. Submenus & Their Purposes

### 5.1 All Sector
**Purpose:** Browse all 50 industry sectors from one place.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Hero Banner | Sector search, filters, Explore Sector CTA |
| Sector Directory | 50+ sectors with icons, company count, news count |
| Trending Sectors | Fastest growing, most read, editor's picks |
| Latest Sector News | Real-time industry news feed |
| Sector Dashboard | Growth, exports, investments, employment, market size |
| Top Companies | Featured company cards |
| Top Leaders | Industry leader cards |
| Featured Reports | Premium intelligence reports |
| Upgrade Banner | Premium Membership and AI insights |

**Revenue:** Report sales, premium memberships, company pages, leader pages, sponsored sectors.
