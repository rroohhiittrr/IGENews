## 1. Module Purpose

Company Pages is the business hub where companies build their digital presence, publish news, showcase products and services, generate leads, and connect with buyers, partners, investors, and industry professionals.

**Page Objective:** Build credibility, increase visibility, generate leads, promote products & services, and encourage subscription upgrades (Free → Verified → Enterprise).

---

## 2. Target Users

Companies (Registered, Verified, Enterprise), Buyers, Investors, Industry Professionals, Government, Media, General Readers.

---

## 3. Mega Menu Page — Screen Sections

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Hero Banner | Company title, search bar, industry/country filters, Register Company, Verify Company, Upgrade to Enterprise CTAs |
| Featured Companies | Enterprise, Verified and Registered company cards with logo, badge, description |
| Company Directory | Searchable listing with filters for industry, country, size, verification |
| Browse by Industry | Industry cards with company count and latest news |
| Company Spotlight | Premium showcase for Enterprise companies with banner, contact CTA, branding |
| Trending Companies | Most viewed and followed companies |
| Featured Enterprise Carousel | Premium branding carousel for Enterprise subscribers |
| Success Stories | Testimonials, achievements, partner success stories |
| Upgrade Banner | Premium benefits: verification, featured placement, analytics, lead generation |
| Newsletter | Email subscription |
| Footer | Quick links to Company Pages, News, Plans, Contact, Help |

---

## 4. Submenus & Purposes

### 4.1 Company Pages
**Purpose:** Company's professional digital profile to build trust and generate leads.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Hero Section | Logo, Banner, Company Name, Verified Badge, Industry, Location |
| Company Overview | About, Mission, Vision, Years in Business |
| Products & Services | Catalogue, Gallery, Brochure |
| Leadership Team | Key executives |
| Company Gallery | Visual brand showcase |
| Business Information | Registration, contacts, social links |
| Lead Generation | Contact, Quote, Meeting, Inquiry CTAs |
| Premium CTA | Upgrade to Verified / Enterprise |

**Revenue:** Premium company pages, featured placement, analytics, lead generation.

---

### 4.2 Company News
**Purpose:** Companies publish announcements, launches, press releases and updates.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Search & Filters | Keyword, sector, date |
| Featured News | Sponsored featured stories |
| Latest News Feed | Chronological company updates |
| Trending News | Most-read company stories |
| Publish Company News CTA | Drives publishing premium |

**Revenue:** Sponsored news, featured stories, premium publishing.

---

### 4.3 By Sector
**Purpose:** Discover companies by industry and compare businesses.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Industry Search | Company discovery |
| Sector Cards | Industry categories with company counts |
| Top Companies | Featured companies per sector |
| Industry Insights | Sector intelligence snippets |
| Register Business CTA | New company onboarding |

**Revenue:** Featured companies, sponsored sectors, premium listings.

---

### 4.4 All Sectors
**Purpose:** Display every industry available on the platform.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Industry Directory | Complete sector listing |
| Search & Filters | Advanced discovery |
| Featured Sectors | Sponsored sector promotion |
| Industry Statistics | Sector metrics |
| Upgrade CTA | Premium membership conversion |

**Revenue:** Sponsored sectors, homepage promotion, premium search.

---

## 5. Subscription Comparison

| Feature | Free | Verified | Enterprise |
|---------|------|----------|------------|
| Company Page | Basic | Enhanced | Premium |
| Company News | Limited | Unlimited | Unlimited + Sponsored |
| Featured Placement | No | Limited | Homepage |
| Analytics | No | Basic | Advanced |
| Lead Generation | Basic | Advanced | CRM |

## 7. Business Value

> Visitors discover trusted companies → Companies generate leads and build credibility → IGEN platform increases recurring subscription revenue through upgrades and premium placements.
