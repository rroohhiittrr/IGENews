## 1. Module Purpose

The Trending module helps users discover the most engaging trade news based on social engagement metrics (likes, shares, comments). It increases platform engagement, drives premium subscriptions, and creates advertising and sponsored content opportunities.

**Objective:** Help users discover the most engaging trade news while increasing engagement, premium subscriptions, advertising opportunities, and sponsored content visibility.

## 2. Submenu Structure

| Metric | Submenu | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| Most Liked | My Sectors | Highest-liked news from user's selected sectors |
| Most Liked | All Sectors | Most-liked news across all sectors |
| Most Shared | My Sectors | Most-shared articles from selected sectors |
| Most Shared | All Sectors | Most-shared stories across all industries |
| Most Commented | My Sectors | Articles with highest discussion in selected sectors |

## 4. Submenu-Specific Purposes & Revenue Focus

### 4.1 Most Liked → My Sectors
**Purpose:** Shows the highest-liked news from the user's selected sectors.
**Revenue Focus:** AI recommendations, Top liked companies, Premium AI ranking.

### 4.2 Most Liked → All Sectors
**Purpose:** Shows the most-liked news across all sectors.
**Revenue Focus:** Global leaderboard, Sponsored rankings, Premium analytics.

### 4.3 Most Shared → My Sectors
**Purpose:** Shows the most-shared articles from selected sectors.
**Revenue Focus:** Viral reports, Featured companies, Premium insights.

### 4.4 Most Shared → All Sectors
**Purpose:** Shows the most-shared stories across industries.
**Revenue Focus:** Country-wise sharing data, Sponsored viral campaigns.

### 4.5 Most Commented → My Sectors
**Purpose:** Shows articles with the highest discussion activity in user's sectors.
**Revenue Focus:** Expert discussions, Community polls, Premium community access.

### 4.6 Most Commented → All Sectors
**Purpose:** Shows the most discussed stories platform-wide.
**Revenue Focus:** Industry debates, Sponsored discussions.

## 6. User Journey

```
Trending Menu → Select Submenu (Most Liked/Shared/Commented) 
→ Apply Filters (Sector/Country/Date)
→ Read News → View AI Insights → Hit AI Lock → Upgrade Prompt
→ Join Discussion → Share/Save → Upgrade to Premium
```
