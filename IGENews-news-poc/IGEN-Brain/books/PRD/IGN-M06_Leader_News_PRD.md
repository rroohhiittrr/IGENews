## 1. Module Purpose

Leader News is the central landing page for Leadership Intelligence. It helps users discover executive news, leadership insights, rankings, interviews, and premium intelligence while driving Verified and Enterprise upgrades.

---

## 2. Business Objectives

| Objective | Mechanism |
|-----------|-----------|
| Increase Verified Leader subscriptions | Upgrade CTAs throughout |
| Increase Enterprise subscriptions | Enterprise-gated features |
| Promote Featured Leader placements | Paid leader spotlight |
| Drive PR & Interview bookings | Executive interview scheduling |
| Promote AI Intelligence Reports | AI-locked content previews |
| Generate advertising revenue | Sponsored stories & display ads |

## 5. SME & ASME Editorial Coverage (Premium Feature)

**Available For:** Verified (Higher Tier) & Enterprise Leaders

**What Users Can See:**
- Up to 1–2 professionally written articles per month
- Articles published by iGEN SMEs & ASMEs
- Leadership achievements and business milestones
- Industry insights and success stories
- Verified Editorial Badge
- Author information (SME/ASME)

**Eligibility:**
- Active Verified (Higher Tier) or Enterprise subscription
- Must meet iGEN's editorial and quality guidelines

**Why:** Provides credible third-party editorial coverage, strengthens the leader's professional reputation, improves SEO and visibility, and encourages upgrades to higher-tier subscription plans.
