## [THE FOUR DASHBOARD SECTIONS]

---

### Section 1 — My Dashboard
**Your Command Centre for Structured Trade Intelligence**

The My Dashboard section is your personalised home within IGEN — showing you only what you've chosen to follow, organised exactly the way you want.

**My Followed Sectors**
All the sectors you've chosen to follow — displayed with their current activity level, latest articles, and sector ranking. See at a glance which of your sectors is most active today and which has developments waiting for your attention.

**My Industries**
The specific industries within your sectors that you've chosen to follow — organised and prioritised. Track which industries are generating the most activity, which have new articles since your last visit, and which are spiking in engagement.

**My Countries**
All the bilateral trade relationships you're monitoring — each showing latest developments, policy updates, and country momentum score. Your export markets and supply-country relationships, all in one organised view.

**My Leaders**
The specific leaders — across 25 designation categories — that you've chosen to follow. See their latest activities, published insights, and mentions in IGEN articles. Your curated leadership intelligence feed.

**My Alerts**
All your configured alert parameters — sector spikes, country policy changes, leader announcements, custom keyword triggers, and AI Plus monitoring signals (if subscribed). Manage, adjust, and review all alerts from one interface.

---

### Section 2 — My Activity
**A Structured Record of Your IGEN Engagement**

My Activity is your personal engagement archive — a structured record of how you've engaged with IGEN's content over time. This is not just a history log. It is a professional intelligence trail that reveals the shape of your trade interests and how they evolve.

**My Likes**
Every article you've liked on IGEN — organised by date, sector, and industry. A structured record of the content you've validated as most relevant. Shareable and usable as a professional bookmark layer.

**My Comments**
Every discussion contribution you've made across IGEN's content — industry threads, country discussions, leader article debates. Your professional voice, archived and accessible.

**My Shares**
Articles you've shared — internally, via link, or to professional networks. A record of the intelligence you've found worth amplifying and the trade topics you're actively engaged with.

**My Bookmarks**
Your organised bookmark library — articles saved for future reference, research, and professional use. Filter by sector, country, industry, or date. Export to brief format (Pro Reader).

**My Reading History**
A structured reading trail — every article you've engaged with, organised by sector, country, and date. Useful for research reference, professional portfolio purposes, and understanding your own evolving trade intelligence interests.

### Section 3 — My Contributions
**Your Professional Presence on IGEN**

My Contributions is the section for IGEN members who are not just consuming intelligence but creating it — Emerging Leaders publishing insights, Corporate members posting announcements, and professionals building their IGEN professional identity.

**My Published Insights**
All the structured industry insights you've published on IGEN — organised by date, sector, engagement received, and views. Your permanent, SEO-indexed thought leadership archive.

**My Drafts**
Articles and insights in progress — save drafts, return to them, refine and edit before submitting for editorial review. Your publishing workspace within IGEN.

**My Leader Page**
A direct link to your dedicated leader profile at indiaglobalnews.com/leaders/your-name — showing how it appears to other readers, your latest published insights, your verification status, and your engagement metrics. (Emerging Leader plan and above.)

**My Corporate Page**
A direct link to your company's structured corporate profile at indiaglobalnews.com/company/your-company — showing the public view of your corporate presence, your sector placement, and your editorial mentions. (Corporate plan.)

**My Engagement Score**
A composite score reflecting your overall professional engagement on IGEN — combining article reading frequency, comment quality, community participation, published insights, and follower growth. A structured measure of your IGEN professional presence.

### Section 4 — My Analytics (Pro+)
**Measure Your Professional Intelligence Footprint**

For Emerging Leader and Corporate members, My Analytics provides structured data on the reach and impact of your IGEN professional presence — turning activity into measurable professional intelligence.

**Engagement Stats**
How many readers are engaging with your published insights — reads, likes, shares, comments, and bookmarks. Article-level and cumulative analytics.

**Article Reach**
The geographic, sectoral, and designation-level reach of your published insights — which sectors is your writing reaching? Which countries? Which leadership designations are reading your insights?

**Follower Count**
How many IGEN readers are following your leader profile — a measure of your professional authority building within the platform.

**Sector Influence**
A structured measure of your influence within your primary sector — based on citation frequency, engagement with your content, and the professional seniority of your readers.

**Reputation Score**
Your overall IGEN professional reputation indicator — combining follower growth, content engagement, publishing consistency, community participation, and editorial feature frequency. A holistic measure of your authority build on IGEN.

## [FAQ SECTION]
### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is MY TRADENEWS?**
Your personal dashboard within IGEN — organising all your followed sectors, industries, countries, and leaders in one place, tracking your activity and contributions, and (for paid plans) providing analytics on your professional presence.

**Is MY TRADENEWS available to free users?**
The My Dashboard section (follows and basic activity) is available to all registered users. Advanced features — unlimited follows, bookmarks library, analytics, leader page, and contribution tracking — require Pro Reader or above.

**How do I set up my personalised dashboard?**
Create a free account, follow your first sector and industries, and your personalised dashboard activates immediately. Each additional follow enhances your dashboard's relevance.

**What is the Engagement Score?**
A composite measure of your IGEN professional engagement — combining reading frequency, comment quality, community participation, publishing activity, and follower metrics. It reflects your overall professional presence on the platform.

**Can I export my bookmarks?**
Pro Reader and above can export bookmarks as a structured PDF brief — useful for research compilation, meeting preparation, and professional reference.

**What is the Reputation Score?**
Available to Emerging Leader and Corporate members — a holistic measure of your IGEN authority build, combining follower growth, content engagement, publishing consistency, and editorial recognition.

**Does IGEN share my activity data with third parties?**
No. Your MY TRADENEWS data — reading history, follows, bookmarks, comments, and analytics — is private to your account. IGEN does not sell individual reader data.

**Can I have separate dashboards for different professional roles?**
Currently, each IGEN account has one dashboard. Corporate accounts can have multiple user accounts — each with their own dashboard configuration.

**What happens to my dashboard if I downgrade my plan?**
Your follows and bookmarks are preserved, but your access to features beyond your new plan level is restricted until you upgrade again. Your data is never deleted when you change plans.

**How do My Analytics help my professional development?**
Analytics show you which of your insights are resonating, which sectors your readers are in, and how your professional authority is growing over time on IGEN — helping you refine your thought leadership strategy.
