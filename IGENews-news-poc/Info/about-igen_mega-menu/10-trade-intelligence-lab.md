### Four Research Intelligence Products That Generate Trust — and Revenue

### Pillar 1 — Industry Reports
**Quarterly Structured Intelligence Documents**

IGEN's industry reports are not recaps of news articles. They are structured intelligence documents — synthesising three months of sectoral, bilateral, and leadership data into a coherent, usable reference document for trade professionals, corporate strategists, and policymakers.

**Report Types:**

**Quarterly Sector Reports**
Each of IGEN's 20 sectors receives a quarterly report covering: sector performance overview, key bilateral trade signals, top industries driving growth, export/import trend analysis, risk factors, policy environment, leader movements, and forward outlook. 15–30 pages. Downloadable PDF. Pro Reader and above.

**Industry Intelligence Briefs**
Focused reports on specific industries within a sector — targeted at specialists who need depth on a single industry, not overview-level sector coverage. Available by request or subscription.

**Country Intelligence Reports**
Structured bilateral trade intelligence reports — covering India's trade relationship with a specific country in depth. Policy environment, sector flows, investment trends, key leaders, and opportunity mapping.

**Risk Analysis Reports**
Sector and country risk assessments — covering policy risk, geopolitical trade risk, supply chain vulnerability, regulatory uncertainty, and early warning signals. Monthly snapshots. Quarterly deep dives.

**Export Opportunity Maps**
Structured opportunity documents — sector-specific export opportunity analysis for specific bilateral relationships, identifying under-penetrated markets, competitive advantages, and entry pathways.

### Pillar 2 — Rankings
**Validated Intelligence That Creates Reference Points**

Rankings create authority. When IGEN publishes a validated ranking of the fastest-growing industries, the most active trade partners, or the most influential corporate leaders, that ranking becomes a reference document — cited, shared, discussed, and used to drive business decisions.

**Rankings IGEN Publishes:**

**Industry Growth Ranking**
Quarterly ranking of IGEN's 1,000 industries by trade intelligence signals — article volume growth, reader engagement acceleration, export signal strength, and bilateral trade mention frequency.

**Leader Ranking**
The most influential trade professionals on IGEN — ranked by the Leader Momentum Index. Published monthly, with a comprehensive annual edition.

**Corporate Ranking**
Companies gaining the most trade intelligence signals across IGEN's content universe — expansion moves, bilateral trade mentions, leadership announcements, and reader engagement.

**Sector Activity Ranking**
All 20 sectors ranked by intelligence intensity — the professional and corporate activity signals that indicate which sectors are India's most dynamic trade environments.

**Country Trade Ranking**
195 bilateral relationships ranked by trade momentum — combining article volume, reader engagement, export signal strength, and policy activity. Published quarterly with annual comprehensive edition.

### Pillar 3 — Surveys and Polls
**Professional Sentiment — The Data That News Cannot Provide**

News tells you what happened. Survey data tells you what professionals think — and what they plan to do. IGEN's professional reader community is a uniquely valuable survey panel: senior trade professionals, exporters, analysts, and corporate leaders across 20 sectors and 195 countries.

**Survey Types:**

**Industry Sentiment Poll**
Monthly pulse check — how optimistic are professionals in each sector about the next 90 days? Sector-by-sector sentiment analysis. Published and shared across IGEN.

**Export Outlook Survey**
Quarterly survey of IGEN's exporter community — current export activity, next-quarter intentions, market-specific optimism, risk concerns, and policy impact assessment.

**Leader Confidence Index**
How confident are IGEN's leader community (FLC and Emerging Leaders) in their sector's growth trajectory? A structured leadership sentiment indicator published quarterly.

**Corporate Outlook Survey**
A targeted survey of IGEN's Corporate Plan members — strategic priorities, market expansion plans, hiring signals, and sector investment intentions for the next year.

**Annual Trade Survey**
IGEN's flagship annual research instrument — a comprehensive survey of the entire IGEN professional community on the state of India's trade, sector performance, bilateral trade priorities, and 12-month outlook. The most comprehensive trade professional sentiment data available in Indian trade media.

### Pillar 4 — Whitepapers
**Where Corporate Intelligence Becomes Structured Research**

Whitepapers represent IGEN's highest-value content product — and its highest-ticket monetisation opportunity. A whitepaper is a long-form structured research document produced under the IGEN intelligence framework, with corporate or institutional sponsorship.

**Whitepaper Types:**

**Sponsored Reports**
A corporate entity commissions IGEN to produce a structured intelligence report on a specific sector, industry, bilateral relationship, or trade topic. IGEN maintains editorial control. The corporate entity is listed as sponsor — not author. The report carries IGEN's credibility and research infrastructure.

**Corporate Whitepapers**
A Corporate Plan member contributes their internal expertise to a structured whitepaper produced in partnership with IGEN's research team. Co-branded. Indexed on IGEN under the corporate profile. Google-ranked under sector + industry + topic keywords.

**Trade Outlook Books**
Annual comprehensive intelligence publications on IGEN's key sectors or bilateral trade zones — combining reporting, data synthesis, expert interviews, and structured analysis. High-production-value documents suitable for corporate gifting, conference distribution, and institutional reference.

**Annual Intelligence Book**
IGEN's flagship annual publication — a comprehensive structured review of India's trade landscape across 20 sectors, 195 countries, and 25 leadership categories. The most authoritative annual trade intelligence publication in India.

**Download Centre**
All published whitepapers, reports, and rankings are accessible in IGEN's structured Download Centre — organised by sector, country, industry, and date. Pro Reader access required for premium reports.

## [FAQ SECTION]
### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Trade Intelligence Lab?**
IGEN's research authority layer — publishing quarterly reports, structured rankings, professional sentiment surveys, and sponsored whitepapers that convert IGEN's daily content intelligence into long-form reference documents.

**How is a Quarterly Sector Report different from a news article?**
A news article covers a single development. A Quarterly Sector Report synthesises three months of developments across a sector — trade flows, policy changes, leadership movements, corporate activity, and risk signals — into a structured 15–30 page intelligence document.

**Are the rankings validated?**
Yes. IGEN's rankings are based on composite intelligence signals from across the platform — article volume, reader engagement, export/import signals, and editorial assessment. Methodology is documented with each published ranking.

**What is a Sponsored Report?**
A structured intelligence research document commissioned by a corporate or institutional entity and produced by IGEN's research team — with IGEN maintaining editorial control and the sponsor listed transparently.

**Can I commission a whitepaper as a Corporate Plan member?**
Yes. Corporate Plan members have access to co-branded intelligence brief and whitepaper opportunities through IGEN's research team. Contact IGEN's corporate team to discuss scope and pricing.

**What is the Annual Trade Survey?**
IGEN's flagship annual survey of the professional community — covering sector performance, bilateral trade priorities, outlook, and policy impact across all 20 sectors and key bilateral relationships. The most comprehensive trade professional sentiment study available in Indian trade media.

**How often are rankings published?**
The Industry Growth Ranking and Leader Ranking are published monthly, with comprehensive annual editions. The Corporate Ranking, Sector Activity Ranking, and Country Trade Ranking are published quarterly.

**Are export opportunity maps available for all 195 countries?**
Yes — as commissioned research. Standard export opportunity maps are available for India's top 50 trade partners as part of IGEN's regular report schedule. Custom maps for specific bilateral relationships can be commissioned.

**Is the Annual Intelligence Book available for purchase?**
Yes. The Annual Intelligence Book is available as a premium purchase for non-IGEN subscribers, and included in the subscription for Pro Reader and above. Contact IGEN for institutional pricing.

**What makes IGEN survey data different from other trade surveys?**
IGEN's survey panel is its professional reader community — trade professionals actively engaged with structured trade intelligence daily. This is a more targeted, relevant panel than general business surveys.
