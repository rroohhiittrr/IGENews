## 1. Executive Summary

The IGEN EOI (Expression of Interest) Form System is a multi-step, conversion-optimised lead capture and qualification engine for the IGEN World platform. It covers **all 15 products** across three product groups — IGEN NEWS, IGEN EXPO, and IGEN Common Products — and is designed to capture high-intent, qualified leads from India's business ecosystem before and at platform launch.

The system consists of:
- A **Landing Page** (EOI Landing Page) with a hero section, trust elements, product selector, multi-step form, and a post-submission journey
- A **4-Step Form Flow** that collects progressively deeper information from applicants
- **Product-specific form fields** for all 15 products (Step 3)
- A **Thank You / Confirmation Screen** with next steps, share actions, and reference ID
- A **Rohit-specific version** of the entire form flow (used for Rohit's personal lead campaigns)
- A **Sales EOI version** (7-step, enhanced flow for sales team use)
- A **quick Enquiry Form** as a fallback for confused/undecided visitors

## 5. EOI Form Module — Multi-Step Flow

### 5.1 Form Stages

The `EOIFormModule` manages 7 distinct UI stages:

```
select → info → step1 → step2 → step3 → step4 → done
```

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| `select` | Product selection screen + EnquiryForm fallback |
| `info` | Product Info Panel — benefits, FOMO, CTA |
| `step1` | Contact details (20 seconds) |
| `step2` | Business details |
| `step3` | Product-specific questions |
| `step4` | Goals, referral, consent |
| `done` | Thank You Screen |

### 5.2 Product Selector (`ProductSelector.tsx`)

- Shows all 15 products grouped into 3 platform groups
- Each product card shows: icon, product name, platform badge, FOMO text
- On selection → transitions to Product Info Panel
- URL param support for pre-selected products (`preselectedProduct`)
- City and Sector pre-fill via URL params (`prefillCity`, `prefillSector`)

### 5.3 Product Info Panel (`ProductInfoPanel.tsx`)

Displayed between product selection and Step 1. Shows:
- Product icon + name
- Platform badge (IGEN NEWS / IGEN EXPO / IGEN COMMON)
- Headline
- 6 key benefit bullet points
- FOMO message
- Primary CTA button: "Begin Your Application"
- Back button: "← Change Product"

### 5.4 Progress Bar (`ProgressBar.tsx`)

- Displays across Steps 1–4 and Done screen
- Shows 4 steps: "You" → "Business" → "Details" → "Goals"
- Each step has icon, label, and completion state
- Fully submitted state shows all steps as complete with a checkmark

## 11. Data Storage & Submission

### localStorage Keys

| Key | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `igen_eoi_current` | Current in-progress submission data |
| `igen_eoi_all_submissions` | Array of all completed submissions |
| `igen_eoi_product` | Last selected product ID |
| `igen_eoi_step` | Last completed step |
| `igen_eoi_enquiries` | Quick Enquiry Form submissions |

### Submission Flow

1. **Step 1 complete** → saved to `localStorage.igen_eoi_current`
2. **Step 2 complete** → merged and saved to `localStorage.igen_eoi_current`
3. **Step 3 complete** → merged and saved to `localStorage.igen_eoi_current`
4. **Step 4 complete + Final Submit** →
   - Sets `fully_submitted: true`
   - Sets `submitted_at` timestamp
   - Appended to `igen_eoi_all_submissions`
   - Sent to `WEBHOOK_URL` (backend webhook endpoint — configurable)
   - Sent to `CRM_WEBHOOK` (CRM integration endpoint — configurable)
   - Console logged for debugging
5. **Thank You Screen** shown with submission data

### Submission ID Format
```
IGEN-{unix_timestamp}-{4_digit_random}
```
Example: `IGEN-1750001234-4823`

### Validation Summary

| Step | Validated Fields |
|---|---|
| Step 1 | full_name (required), mobile (10-digit), email (format), city (required), interested_in (required) |
| Step 2 | company_name, industry_sector, designation, business_category, years_in_business |
| Step 3 | Product-specific required fields (varies per product — see Section 6.3) |
| Step 4 | why_interested (min 20 chars), referral_source, consent (must be true) |

## 20. Key Business Rules

1. **No Payment Required** — EOI is completely free. The form must never suggest any payment.
2. **Founding Member FOMO** — Every product has a limited-slot founding member message. This must be preserved in all versions.
3. **24/48 Hour Response Commitment** — The team commits to contacting leads within 24 hours (most products) or 48 hours (international/conference/awards).
4. **Rohit Attribution** — All leads from Rohit's version must be tagged with `team_member: "Rohit"` in the submission meta.
5. **Product Switch Allowed** — Users can change their product selection in Step 1's dropdown, which overrides the pre-selected product.
6. **Auto-Save** — Progress is saved after each step to localStorage. Users can return later.
7. **Multi-Product Interest** — Step 4 allows users to express interest in multiple products in a single EOI.
8. **Conditional Fields** — Viksit Bharat Conference shows "Speaking topic" and "Sponsorship budget" fields only when those roles are selected.
9. **Consent Mandatory** — Form cannot be submitted without ticking the consent checkbox.
