## [FAQ SECTION]
### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between FPC, FLC, and FCC?**
FPC (Founding Professionals Community) is for Pro Reader members — the first 500. FLC (Founding Leaders Community) is for Emerging Leader members — the first 100. FCC (Founding Corporate Community) is for Corporate members — the first 20.

**Can I be part of more than one founding circle?**
No. Each founding circle is tied to a specific plan level. You belong to the circle corresponding to your plan.

**What happens after the founding slots fill?**
New subscribers continue to join IGEN at the standard price — but founding member status, founding wall listing, lifetime price protection, and founding circle badges are not available after the slots are filled. Ever.

**Is the Founding Wall permanent?**
Yes. IGEN's Founding Wall — listing all 500 FPC professionals, 100 FLC Charter Leaders, and 20 FCC Corporate Partners — is a permanent, SEO-indexed page on indiaglobalnews.com. It does not expire.

**Can founding members lose their status?**
Founding status and price protection are maintained as long as subscription remains active. Members who lapse and rejoin pay the prevailing rate and do not regain founding status.

**What is the Quarterly Virtual Meet for FPC?**
A closed-door virtual session with IGEN's founder, senior team, and special guests — exclusively for FPC Founding Professionals. Agenda includes intelligence briefings, platform updates, community highlights, and professional networking.

**Who is invited to the Annual Founding Leader Roundtable?**
All 100 FLC Charter Leaders. The roundtable brings IGEN's entire founding leadership community together — with IGEN leadership, sector experts, and policy guests. Format is hybrid or virtual depending on year.

**Is the FCC recognition visible to general readers?**
Yes. The FCC seal is displayed on corporate profile pages and visible to all IGEN readers. The Founding Corporate Partner status is part of IGEN's permanent institutional recognition architecture.

**Can I suggest industry coverage as a community member?**
Yes. All IGEN community members can use the "Suggest Industry Coverage" function — a direct feedback channel to IGEN's editorial team. FPC, FLC, and FCC members have priority consideration in the editorial response.

**Are community discussions moderated?**
Yes. All community discussions are moderated by IGEN's team for professional standards, factual accuracy, and community guidelines compliance. IGEN does not allow spam, promotional content, or personal attacks in community spaces.
