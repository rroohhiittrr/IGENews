## [PRICING PHILOSOPHY]
### Why IGEN Doesn't Race to the Bottom

IGEN is premium structured trade intelligence. Not mass media. Not a blog. Not a free news app with ads.

Premium pricing is a deliberate architectural choice:

**High price signals seriousness.** It filters casual browsers from committed professionals. The result is a community of serious trade professionals — which makes the platform more valuable for everyone in it.

**Premium pricing protects editorial quality.** IGEN's revenue model is subscription-driven, not advertising-driven. This means content decisions are made for readers, not for advertisers.

**Founding pricing creates urgency and loyalty.** Early members get permanently lower pricing. This rewards the professionals who believed in IGEN before the crowd arrived.

> "Premium price filters seriousness. Serious members engage more. Engaged members renew." — IGEN Pricing Constitution

## [FAQ SECTION]
### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a free trial for paid plans?**
The Free Plan gives unlimited article reading indefinitely — that is your trial. The moment you hit a follow limit, IGEN shows you exactly what Pro would unlock.

**Can I cancel my paid plan?**
Yes. All plans offer cancellation. Founding Member pricing, however, is only available during the founding access window — once that closes, the anchor price applies on renewal.

**What is a Founding Member?**
The first 500 Pro Readers, first 100 Emerging Leaders, and first 20 Corporates to join IGEN at paid tiers. They receive permanently locked pricing, founding recognition, and community badges.

**Is the Emerging Leader Plan worth it if I'm not well-known?**
That is precisely who it's designed for. The leaders who benefit most are those who start building digital authority before they achieve widespread recognition. Reputation compounds over time.

**What happens when the Founding Member slots fill?**
The anchor price activates. Pro Reader becomes ₹39,999/year. Emerging Leader becomes ₹99,999/year. Corporate becomes ₹4,00,000/year and above.

**Are there any discounts after Founding pricing closes?**
No. IGEN does not offer public discounts — it degrades premium perception. Upgrade features and extended access may occasionally be offered, but the price anchor is maintained.

**What is the Corporate Plan best suited for?**
Export houses, large and mid-market SMEs, trade bodies, sector associations, and enterprise teams who need structured trade intelligence, multi-user access, and structured corporate visibility on India's leading trade intelligence platform.

**Can I upgrade my plan at any time?**
Yes. You can upgrade from Free to Pro, or Pro to Emerging Leader, at any time. Founding pricing is only available during the founding access window.

**Does the Corporate Plan include advertising on IGEN?**
No. IGEN is not an advertising platform. Corporate Plan visibility is structural — profile pages, sector placement, spotlight features, and co-branded briefs — within IGEN's editorial-independent framework.

**What is the difference between the Corporate Plan tiers?**
Base, Mid, and Premium Corporate tiers vary by number of user seats, number of sectors covered, visibility features, and co-branding scope. Schedule a consultation with IGEN's corporate team for a custom proposal.
